# Bluetooth Debugging Guide

This guide explains how to capture Bluetooth logs when experiencing connection issues with your trainer.

## Requirements

- Project Phoenix installed
- Trainer powered on and advertising as `Vee_*`

## Capture logs from the app

1. Open **Project Phoenix**
2. Go to **Settings → Connection Logs**
3. Reproduce the connection problem
4. Use **Share logs** to send the file from the system share sheet

## Tips

Ensure the trainer is powered on and Bluetooth is enabled on the phone. Stay close to the trainer during the capture.
